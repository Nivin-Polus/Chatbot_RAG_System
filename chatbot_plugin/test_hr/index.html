Leave Policy

Last updated: September 2024

Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR system.

  • Accrual rate: 2.08 days per month
  • Maximum carryover: 5 days to next year
  • Approval required from direct manager

Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave annually. Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

  • No carryover to next year
  • Can be used for family member care
  • Mental health days included

Maternity/Paternity Leave

New parents are entitled to extended leave with job protection and benefits continuation.

  • Maternity: 16 weeks paid leave
  • Paternity: 4 weeks paid leave
  • Additional unpaid leave available
